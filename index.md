    <h2>Policy questions through a household lens</h2>
  </div>
  <article class="research-card research-featured">
    <div class="research-meta"><span>JOB MARKET PAPER</span><span>WITH SIMING YE</span></div>
    <h3>Retire Him, Deploy Her: Couples’ Asymmetric Labor Responses to Pension Eligibility</h3>
    <p>When husbands become pension-eligible in rural China, wives increase labor supply to offset lost earnings. The results show how pension design and formal-employment restrictions can unintentionally shift old-age income risk toward women.</p>
